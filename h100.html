📱 H100 - App 1
App — Caregiver Login to H100 & Add Caregivees
Health100™ enables caregivers to securely manage and support the health needs of minors, adults, and pets through a single, connected platform. From managing caregiving relationships to helping with prescriptions and pharmacy information across CVS Pharmacy®, CVS Caremark® Mail Order Service Pharmacy, and CVS Specialty®.
🌐 H100 - Web 1
Web Caregiving Authorization & Consent
Enable caregivees to review, accept, or decline caregiving request on the web through a secure XID-based authorization flow, improving onboarding completion rates.

Executive Summary Ten features are committed to ship this quarter across July, August, and September — spanning security hardening, app onboarding, caregivee population expansion, and the full H100 web and app caregiving experience. All features shown have a confirmed due date in Q3 2026, regardless of approval status.

July Focus Foundation month — wire the backend identity layer via RxConnect, launch the H100 web authorization flow, and ensure newly onboarded caregivers know their next steps.

-1158

📅 August Deliverables 4
August Focus Expansion month — broaden the caregivee population to Caremark-only users, surface smart plug-and-play suggestions to reduce enrollment friction, and ship the full H100 mobile app caregiving experience.

-853
Surfaces intelligent suggestions for potential caregivees within the CVSH experience. Due Aug 7

-852

MFA/MNO Enforcement — Adult Caregiver Flow

H100 App — Universal Caregiving
-1156
Bringing the complete universal caregiving experience to the H100 mobile app surface. Due Aug 31
